🛡️ Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

At SubWatch, developed by Mark Varga, we believe your personal finances are your private business. Our application is engineered from the ground up to operate offline-first and on-device without harvesting, selling, or transmitting your data.

Summary for App Store Review: SubWatch collects NO personal data, employs NO advertising or tracking SDKs, and stores 100% of your subscription entries locally in an encrypted SwiftData container on your iPhone.

1. What Data We Collect

None. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers.

  • No Account Required: SubWatch requires no registration, email address, password, or login credentials.
  • No Banking Credentials: SubWatch never prompts for bank logins, credit card numbers, or third-party bank linking.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: SubWatch contains zero telemetry, analytics frameworks (e.g. Google Firebase, Meta SDK, AppsFlyer, Adjust), or third-party ad networks.

2. Local On-Device Data Storage

All subscription records, prices, renewal dates, categories, icons, and currency preferences are saved directly on your physical iPhone using Apple's SwiftData framework. Data remains isolated in the application sandbox and is protected by iOS device-level encryption.

3. Payment Reminders & Notifications

SubWatch schedules notification alerts locally on your device via Apple's native UserNotifications framework. No notification payload is sent across the internet.

4. In-App Purchases (StoreKit 2)

Transactions for SubWatch Pro are processed exclusively by Apple. We never see or store your payment details. Apple provides only cryptographically signed receipts to verify your purchase status on-device.

5. Data Deletion

You can erase all saved data at any time inside the app via Settings → Erase All Data. Deleting the app also immediately deletes all stored data.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact: vargamark@icloud.com.
