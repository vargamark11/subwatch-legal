🛡️ Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

At SubWatch, your financial privacy is fundamental. The application was designed from day one to operate entirely on your device without harvesting, selling, or transmitting your personal or financial data.

Key Takeaway: SubWatch never asks for your bank login credentials, never requires account registration, and stores all subscription records exclusively on your local iPhone storage.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

  • No Accounts: You do not need an email, phone number, or password to use SubWatch.
  • No Bank Linking: SubWatch does not integrate with Plaid, Yodlee, or third-party open banking APIs. You manually record and control all subscription details.
  • No Advertising or Tracking SDKs: There are no advertising trackers, analytics trackers (such as Google Firebase, Meta SDK, Adjust, or AppsFlyer), or diagnostic profiling embedded in the app.

2. 100% Local On-Device Persistence

All subscription entries, service names, recurring billing dates, prices, categories, and currency selections are saved locally on your iPhone using Apple's native SwiftData framework inside your sandboxed app container. Your data never touches any external server operated by us.

3. Local Notifications

Payment reminders are scheduled and triggered 100% locally on your iPhone via Apple's UserNotifications framework. No remote push notifications or cloud notification dispatchers are used.

4. In-App Purchases & StoreKit 2

SubWatch Pro purchases and subscriptions are handled directly and securely by Apple using StoreKit 2. We never have access to your credit card, Apple Cash, or billing address. Apple provides only cryptographically signed verification tokens directly to the device to unlock premium features.

💬 Support & FAQ

Need help, have feedback, or found an issue? We're committed to making SubWatch the best subscription tracker for iPhone.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I restore my SubWatch Pro purchase?
    Open SubWatch → go to Settings → tap SubWatch Pro → tap Restore Purchases. Your purchase will be verified instantly via StoreKit 2.
  • How do I change the display currency?
    Open SubWatch → go to Settings → tap Currency, then select EUR, USD, GBP, HUF, CHF, or your preferred currency.
  • How do payment reminders work?
    Reminders are sent locally based on your preferred timing (1, 2, 3, or 7 days before payment). Ensure notifications are allowed for SubWatch in iOS Settings → Notifications.

🗑️ Account & Data Deletion

In full compliance with App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v):

  • Because SubWatch requires no accounts, passwords, or cloud servers, there is no remote account to delete.
  • You can completely wipe all local data and custom subscriptions at any time directly inside the app: navigate to Settings → Erase All Data.
  • Deleting the SubWatch app from your iPhone immediately and permanently removes all stored data from your device sandbox.
