Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I restore my SubWatch Pro purchase?
If you reinstall SubWatch or switch to a new iPhone, your purchase is never lost. Open SubWatch → go to Settings → tap SubWatch Pro → tap Restore Purchases. Your Apple ID will verify the license via StoreKit 2 and unlock your features immediately without charging you again.
2. How do I cancel an auto-renewing subscription?
Subscriptions on iOS are securely managed by Apple. To change or cancel your subscription at any time:
1. Open your iPhone Settings app.
2. Tap your Apple ID name at the top.
3. Tap Subscriptions.
4. Select SubWatch and choose Cancel Subscription.
3. Why doesn't SubWatch connect to my bank account?
SubWatch is built on a privacy-first philosophy. We intentionally do not use third-party banking aggregators (like Plaid) to ensure your bank credentials and financial history are never transmitted over the internet or stored on external cloud databases. You retain 100% control of your records.
4. Why am I not receiving payment reminder notifications?
Ensure notifications are enabled on your iPhone:
1. Open iPhone Settings → Notifications → SubWatch.
2. Ensure Allow Notifications is toggled ON.
3. Inside SubWatch, verify that reminders are enabled under Settings → Notifications.
5. How do I change the display currency?
Open SubWatch → navigate to Settings → tap Currency. Choose EUR (€), USD ($), GBP (£), HUF (Ft), CHF, CAD, AUD, or other global currencies. All totals, monthly projections, and averages will automatically update.
6. How can I delete all my data?
Under Settings → Erase All Data, you can permanently delete all subscriptions and custom records at any time. Deleting the SubWatch app from your device also instantly erases the local SwiftData container.
